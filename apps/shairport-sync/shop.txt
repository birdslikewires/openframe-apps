Shairport Sync makes the OpenFrame an AirPlay 2 speaker. Choose it on an iPhone, iPad or Mac, and the music plays here, on the OpenFrame's own speaker or its USB DAC.

Several speakers can play together in time, so the OpenFrame can join HomePods, other AirPlay speakers or more OpenFrames around the house, all in step.

It appears under the name the OpenFrame has in Settings, under About. It runs in the background, whatever is on the screen.
