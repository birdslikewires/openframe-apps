HamClock displays what the sun and the ionosphere are doing to radio, with solar flux, sunspots, the K index and band conditions, and DX spots if you'd like them.

The first time it starts, HamClock asks a few questions to set itself up. Its keyboard types the upper character when you tap the top half of a key, and the lower one from the bottom half, so a USB keyboard makes this easier.

Alternatively, while it's running you can interact with HamClock in a browser on another computer. Visit your OpenFrame's network address with ":8081/live.html" on the end, such as "http://192.168.1.20:8081/live.html".
