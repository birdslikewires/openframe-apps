HamClock displays what the sun and the ionosphere are doing to radio, with solar flux, sunspots, the K index and band conditions, and DX spots if you'd like them.

The first time it starts, it asks a few questions to set itself up. Its keyboard types the upper character on a key when you tap the top half, and the lower one from the bottom half, so a USB keyboard makes this easier.

While it's running, you can also watch HamClock in a browser on another computer, by going to your OpenFrame's address with :8081/live.html on the end, such as http://192.168.1.20:8081/live.html. The address on its own won't find it.
